How Hearth is different

  • You have the final say

    When someone suggests a change to your medicines, you see exactly what would change and approve or decline it.

  • Reminders that fit your day

    Doses are tied to your routine, like “after breakfast”. Hearth notices when your routine shifts and offers to move with it.

  • You are always told first

    Family and caretakers are only alerted if a dose stays unconfirmed, and only for the medicines you mark as important.

  • Ready for the doctor

    Before each appointment, Hearth prepares a one-page summary with your own questions on it.
